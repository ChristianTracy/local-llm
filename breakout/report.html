What is “Anti-tunneling”? At high speed the ball can skip over a brick between frames (“tunnel”). ✅ sub splits movement into tiny sub-steps (ceil(speed*dt / (radius/2))) and checks collision each step — no tunneling. ➖ clamp only caps dt to 0.05 — safe at normal speed but can still tunnel if very fast. ✕ = no protection.
Footnotes — plain language
  • * gemma4-26A4B — Brick width calculation ignores gap/padding, so 8 bricks overflow the 800 px canvas.
  • † gemma4-E4B — Brick width (800-10)/8 totals 880 px → bricks are clipped off-screen.
  • ‡ gemma4-26A4B — Pressing Space while paused calls launch() instead of resuming, resetting ball direction.
  • § ornith-1.5-9b — checkBallFell() / loseLife() exist but are never called in update(), so the ball falling never costs a life — you can never lose.
  • ¶ ornith-1.5-9b — Score is 10 * (5 - row) (10–50 per brick) instead of the spec’s flat 10 per brick.
